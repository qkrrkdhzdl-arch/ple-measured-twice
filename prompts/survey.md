# Survey

You are the first engineer to open this project folder. Another engineer will
do the full task afterwards; your job is to leave a map that saves them hours.

Inspect the workspace with `bash` and `read_file` only. Do not create, change,
or delete any file, and do not start long calculations.

Work out and report:

1. **The deliverable contract.** Every file or folder the task says will be
   graded, with its exact path, format, and required contents. Quote the task
   line that names it. If the task implies a deliverable without naming a path,
   say which path a grader would most plausibly read and why.
2. **The inputs.** The drawings, specs, sheets, models, emails, and scripts
   present, where they live, and which of them the deliverable depends on.
3. **The method.** The calculation or design procedure the deliverable needs,
   the codes/standards/clauses that appear in the inputs, and the units and
   coordinate systems in play.
4. **The traps.** Ambiguities, contradictions between documents, revision
   dates, values that must be recomputed rather than copied, and any tooling
   (solvers, scripts, MCP apps) the task expects you to use.
5. **A verification recipe.** The concrete checks a reviewer would run on the
   finished deliverable: numbers to recompute, cross-sections to look up,
   geometry constraints to test, and file-format rules.

Reply with the map as compact Markdown under those five headings. Facts with
paths, sheet numbers, and values - not general advice.
